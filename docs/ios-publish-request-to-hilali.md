# Ombi kwa Hilali (Account Holder) / Request to Hilali

## Kiswahili
Habari Hilali. Tunataka kuweka app ya Simulizi za Sauti kwenye App Store chini ya akaunti yako ya Apple Developer ("Hilali Alexander Ruhundwa").
Tafadhali fanya yafuatayo:
1. Ingia https://appstoreconnect.apple.com → **Users and Access** → bofya Amani Abdallah (amongdawiner@gmail.com).
2. Badilisha role kutoka **App Manager** kuwa **Admin**, na hakikisha "Access to Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles" imewashwa. Hifadhi.
3. Ukikubali, Amani atapata ufikiaji wa https://developer.apple.com/account kwa timu yako (atamweza kuunda App ID, vyeti na profile).

## English
Hi Hilali. We want to publish Simulizi za Sauti on the App Store under your Apple Developer account ("Hilali Alexander Ruhundwa").
Please:
1. Sign in at https://appstoreconnect.apple.com → **Users and Access** → click Amani Abdallah (amongdawiner@gmail.com).
2. Change the role from **App Manager** to **Admin** and make sure "Access to Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles" is on. Save.
3. After that, Amani can access https://developer.apple.com/account for your team and create the App ID, certificates and provisioning profile.

Planned iOS bundle ID: `com.simulizi.ruhundwa.ios` (the Android package `com.simulizi.ruhundwa` is already registered under another team).
